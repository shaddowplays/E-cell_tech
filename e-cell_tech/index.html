What we run

Three kinds of sessions, spread across the year, aimed at different stages of having an idea.

Pitch circuits

Monthly, low-stakes practice rounds where you pitch to a small panel and walk away with notes you can actually use.

Build weekends

Forge is the biggest of these — short, intense sprints that turn an idea into a working prototype in under 48 hours.

Founder talks

Alumni and campus founders come back to talk through what actually happened after they left — the parts that don't make the pitch deck.
