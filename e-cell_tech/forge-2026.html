FORGE 2026

Forty-eight hours to turn a rough idea into a real venture — built, not just pitched.

When Feb 6–8, 2026
Where New Auditorium, BITS Hyderabad
Apply to build

24 teams take the floor. One demo day.

What Forge is

Forge is a 48-hour build sprint for BITS Hyderabad students who want to find out if an idea actually holds up. No slide decks on day one — you're talking to users, sketching a business model, and building something you can show by Sunday evening.

Come with a problem you've noticed on campus or beyond, or show up solo and find a team at kickoff. Either way, you leave with a working prototype, a real pitch, and a clearer sense of whether it's worth pursuing past the weekend.

Build, don't just plan

Time is split between structured sessions and open build blocks, with mentors circulating the floor all weekend.

Forge sessions

Short, sharp workshops on talking to users, pricing, and pitching — timed so you can apply them immediately.

Money on the table

₹2,00,000 across three venture tracks, plus a pre-incubation slot with the campus innovation hub.

A real jury

Founders and investors from the E-Cell alumni network judge Demo Day — not just your professors.
