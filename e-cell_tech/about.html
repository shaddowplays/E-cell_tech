The team behind it

E-Cell BITS Hyderabad is entirely student-run — planned, funded, and pulled off by people taking the same courses you are.

What we're actually for

Most students on this campus have an idea they've never said out loud — a tool they wish existed, a business they've sketched on the back of an assignment. E-Cell exists to make saying it out loud less risky.

We run the events, but the actual work is smaller than that: putting a founder two years ahead of you in the same room, giving a half-formed idea a deadline, and making sure the first pitch you ever give isn't the one that decides everything.

How we're organised

Four small teams, each responsible for a different part of getting an event from idea to demo day.

Programming

Designs the actual format of each event — what a team does hour by hour at Forge, what a Pitch Circuit round looks like.

Operations

Books the rooms, runs the budget, and makes sure the midnight lounge actually has coffee in it.

PR & Outreach

Brings in mentors, jury members, and the alumni founders who come back for Founder Talks.

Design & Web

Builds the pages you're looking at right now, along with every poster and form behind them.

Want to help run the next one?
